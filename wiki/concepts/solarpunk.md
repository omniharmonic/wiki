# Solarpunk

## Definition

**Solarpunk** is an aesthetic and political imagination of an abundant future in which technology serves life rather than escape from it. Where cyberpunk extrapolated our worst trajectory — corporate feudalism under perpetual surveillance — solarpunk insists on the other branch: communities living well within living systems, abundance generated from the bottom up rather than hoarded at the top. Above all it is a *positive* image of the future, the very thing a captured imagination has been quietly deprived of.

## A Positive Image of the Future

The contemporary progressive imagination, having been steered into a posture of pure critique, learned to name what is wrong with power but never to propose what should replace it. A critique cannot lead anyone anywhere. Solarpunk is the refusal of that trap — the audacity to imagine that the future could be beautiful, and to organize around what we could build rather than only around who we should blame.

## Transpolitical Imagination

Solarpunk is a *transpolitical* revolutionary imagination. Instead of keeping people at one another's throats over the inventory of wedge issues that divide them while capital operates smoothly through both major parties, it asks a different question: not *who do we blame?* but *what could we build?* What nearly everyone actually wants turns out to be shared across the divide — a healthy place to raise children, dignified work, the means to support those you love and show up for your community. Solarpunk points toward holistic quality of life and [[community]] sovereignty rather than toward left or right.

## Underthrow, Not Overthrow

Solarpunk does not run through the seizure of central power. We do not need to capture the state to build the future; the act of seizing an instrument designed for domination corrupts the thing you set out to create. Instead solarpunk builds [[dual-power]] — parallel institutions that meet real human needs directly, outside and alongside the failing system. Build the alternative until the old order becomes obsolete. Not overthrow but *underthrow*: the patient construction of something so much more alive that people simply migrate toward it.

## In Benjamin's Work

Benjamin develops solarpunk as the through-line of [[the-solarpunk-revolution-is-inevitable]], arguing that the contradictions of late-stage capitalism — accelerated by AI's erosion of labor's leverage — make deep transformation inevitable, and that solarpunk names the branch worth choosing when the rupture comes. It threads his broader commitments to local, regenerative economies, [[post-tragic-audacity]], and building the world we want in the places where we actually live.
