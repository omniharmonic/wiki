# Federated Cooperative Supply Chain

A federated cooperative supply chain is a supply chain in which every business unit along it — producer, processor, distributor, retailer — is cooperatively owned, and those cooperatives then federate to jointly own the infrastructure none of them could afford alone. It is the move that turns [[worker-cooperatives|cooperatives]] from good, ethical businesses into a different *kind* of economy.

The problem it solves is that a lone cooperative embedded in an extractive economy buys from extractive suppliers, borrows from extractive banks, and sells into markets that drain its surplus — becoming, despite its virtues, simply the newest node from which value is extracted. Closure is not a property of individual businesses; it is a property of whole systems. A single cooperative cannot close a loop any more than a single artery can constitute a circulatory system.

Federation closes the loops through "cooperatives cooperating": pooling capital through special purpose vehicles and cooperative banks to collectively own the secondary layer (the processing facility, the aggregation hub, the land trust), held by the federation rather than any single member so that the old chokepoint can never become the new point of extraction. In a conventional chain the game is positional — capture a chokepoint and rent-seek against everyone upstream and down. In a federated chain, profits generated at each scale are shared *across* the scales through intercooperation equity: solidarity funds, multi-class membership (farmers, workers, and consumers all holding a stake), open books, and mutual vesting.

[[networked-cooperativism|Mondragón]] has run this architecture for seventy years in the Basque Country. The pattern is sector-agnostic — food from soil to shelf, housing with portable equity across a network, care cooperatives whose credits transfer between neighbors — and, because its knowledge layer is [[cosmolocalism|cosmolocal]], every federation's governance structures, legal instruments, and coordination software become the inheritance of every federation that follows.
