# Open Council

## Definition

Open Council is a searchable archive of more than 1,500 city council meetings from London, Ontario (2014-2024), created by Heenal Rajani and accessible through a retrieval-augmented generation ([[wiki/concepts/RAG]]) chatbot that indexes meeting transcripts, official minutes, and press reports. Benjamin Life treats it as a working example of civic infrastructure that makes municipal decision-making queryable to ordinary citizens, turning a decade of governance records into a living, interrogable [[wiki/concepts/Knowledge Commons]]. For Benjamin it is less a single product than a proof of concept for AI-mediated knowledge commoning in service of [[wiki/concepts/participatory-democracy]].

## Civic Utility and Democratized Expertise

Benjamin frames Open Council as a [[wiki/concepts/civic-utilities|civic utility]] that democratizes expertise. Where understanding municipal decision-making patterns once required insider familiarity or hours of archival labor, a RAG chatbot lets any resident ask plain-language questions and receive grounded answers drawn from transcripts and minutes. This lowers the threshold for informed [[wiki/concepts/civic-participation]], makes transparency the default rather than the exception, and supplies the enabling substrate for the kind of distributed civic agency he associates with [[wiki/concepts/collective-intelligence]] and engaged citizenship.

## Knowledge Commoning and Federation

The tool operationalizes Benjamin's broader vision of using AI agents for knowledge commoning, combining Whisper transcription, pattern extraction, and federated indexing to preserve civic knowledge for reuse. He envisions Open Council replicating to "every city," with the "collective insights from different cities" able to "talk together" so that agents can mediate cross-jurisdictional learning without imposing universal standards. In this view it becomes foundational infrastructure for bioregional governance, sitting alongside participatory budgeting and [[wiki/concepts/liquid-democracy]] mechanisms as part of an emerging civic innovation ecosystem and connecting to his consulting work through OpenCivics Labs.

## In Benjamin's Work

Benjamin holds up Open Council as exemplary civic infrastructure when articulating how technology can serve a renewed citizenship and an [[the-infrastructure-of-belonging|infrastructure of belonging]]; it concretizes the [[wiki/concepts/civic-utilities|civic utilities]] and participatory ideals he develops in [[citizen]].
